# Security SDLC (SecSDLC) – 13 Marks

## Introduction

**SecSDLC (Security Systems Development Life Cycle)** is the security-focused development process described in the uploaded PDF. It follows the phases of the traditional SDLC but adds activities that are specifically related to **information security**, such as security policy, threat and risk analysis, incident response, disaster recovery, security technology and continuous monitoring.

The SecSDLC consists of **six phases**:

**Investigation → Analysis → Logical Design → Physical Design → Implementation → Maintenance and Change**. a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…

# 1. Investigation Phase

The **Investigation phase** of the SecSDLC begins with a **directive from upper management**.

The directive specifies:

- The **process**
- Expected **outcomes**
- **Goals** of the project
- **Budget**
- Other **constraints**

Frequently, the phase begins with a **statement of program security policy**, which outlines the implementation of a security program within the organization. a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…

During this phase:

- Teams of responsible **managers, employees and contractors** are organized.
- Problems are analyzed.
- The scope of the project is defined.
- Specific goals and objectives are identified.
- An organizational feasibility analysis is performed.

The feasibility analysis determines whether the organization has the **resources and commitment** required to conduct a successful security analysis and design. a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…

---

# 2. Analysis Phase

In the **Analysis phase**, the documents prepared during the Investigation phase are studied.

The development team:

- Conducts a preliminary analysis of existing **security policies and programs**.
- Analyzes documented **current threats and associated controls**.
- Examines relevant **legal issues** that could affect the security solution.
- Performs **risk management**. a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…

### Risk Management

Risk management is the process of:

1. **Identifying** risks.
2. **Assessing** risks.
3. **Evaluating** the levels of risk facing the organization.

It specifically considers threats to the organization's security and to the information stored and processed by the organization. a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…

---

# 3. Logical Design Phase

The **Logical Design phase** creates and develops the **blueprints for security**.

It also examines and implements key policies that influence later security decisions. a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…

An important activity during this phase is planning for incidents and disasters.

### Three important plans

**1. Continuity Planning**

It answers:

> How will the business continue in the event of a loss?

**2. Incident Response**

It answers:

> What do you do when an attack occurs?

**3. Disaster Recovery**

It answers:

> What must you do to recover information and vital systems immediately after a disastrous event?

These questions are examined and the solutions are documented. a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…

A feasibility analysis is then performed to determine whether the project should continue or be outsourced.

---

# 4. Physical Design Phase

In the **Physical Design phase**, the security technology required to support the blueprint created during Logical Design is evaluated.

The activities include:

- Evaluating the required security technology.
- Generating alternative solutions.
- Agreeing upon a final design.
- Revisiting the security blueprint when necessary.
- Preparing criteria for determining a successful solution.
- Designing **physical security measures** to support technological solutions. a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…

At the end of this phase:

- A feasibility study determines the organization's readiness.
- The design is presented to the champion and sponsors.
- The involved parties get an opportunity to approve the project before implementation begins. a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…

---

# 5. Implementation Phase

The **Implementation phase** is similar to the traditional SDLC implementation phase.

During this phase, security solutions are:

- **Acquired**
- **Tested**
- **Implemented**
- **Tested again**

Personnel issues are also evaluated, and specific **training and education programs** are conducted. a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…

Finally, the complete tested package is presented to **upper management for final approval**. a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…

---

# 6. Maintenance and Change Phase

The **Maintenance and Change phase** is important because information security systems require continuous attention.

According to the PDF, today's information security systems need:

- **Constant monitoring**
- **Testing**
- **Modification**
- **Updating**
- **Repairing** a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…

Security requirements can change as threats and organizational requirements change. Therefore, the security system must be continuously maintained and modified.

# SecSDLC – Phase Summary

| Phase | Major Activities |
|---|---|
| **Investigation** | Management directive, security policy, team formation, scope, goals and feasibility |
| **Analysis** | Analyze security policies, threats, controls, legal issues and risks |
| **Logical Design** | Security blueprint, continuity planning, incident response and disaster recovery |
| **Physical Design** | Select security technologies, develop alternatives, physical security and final design |
| **Implementation** | Acquire, test and implement security solutions; training and education |
| **Maintenance & Change** | Continuous monitoring, testing, modification, updating and repairing |

## Conclusion

**SecSDLC** provides a structured approach for developing and maintaining an organization's security system. It begins with **upper management direction and security policy**, proceeds through **analysis, logical design, physical design and implementation**, and finally continues with **maintenance and change**.

The important security-specific activities include **risk analysis, security blueprint development, incident response, business continuity, disaster recovery, security technology selection and continuous monitoring**. Thus, security is considered throughout the entire system development life cycle. a0978e71-136b-470e-8889-ed245dd…
