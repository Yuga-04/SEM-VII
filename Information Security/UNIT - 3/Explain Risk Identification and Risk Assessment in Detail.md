# 1. Risk Identification

**Risk Identification** is the process of finding the **assets, threats, and vulnerabilities** that may cause harm to an organization.

It helps the organization understand **what needs protection and what can go wrong**. Information_Security_Unit_III (…

### A. Asset Identification

An **asset** is anything valuable to the organization that needs protection.

Examples include:

- People
- Procedures
- Data
- Software
- Hardware
- Network components
- Information systems

### B. Asset Valuation

After identifying assets, their **value or importance** is determined.

Factors considered include:

- Importance of the asset
- Revenue generated
- Replacement cost
- Effect on the organization if the asset is lost
- Criticality of the asset

- Hardware failure
- Software failure
- Natural disasters
- Technological changes or obsolescence

# 2. Risk Assessment

**Risk Assessment** is the process of determining the **level of risk** associated with identified threats and vulnerabilities.

It helps the organization decide **which risks are more serious and need immediate attention**. Information_Security_Unit_III (…

### A. Determine Likelihood

Likelihood means the **probability that a vulnerability will be successfully exploited**.

It can be represented using values such as:

- **0.1 → Low likelihood**
- **1.0 → High likelihood**

Information_Security_Unit_III (…

### B. Determine Risk

The identified information is used to calculate the level of risk.

The study material gives the following form:

**Risk = Likelihood × Asset Value − Risk Already Controlled + Uncertainty**

For example:

- Asset Value = 50
- Likelihood = 1.0
- Existing Control = 0%
- Uncertainty = 10%

Therefore:

**Risk = 1.0 × 50 + 10% = 55**

### C. Assign Risk Rating

After calculating the risk, it is given a **risk rating**.

For example:

- Low Risk
- Medium Risk
- High Risk

High-risk areas should receive greater attention and stronger security controls.

# Difference Between Risk Identification and Risk Assessment

| Risk Identification | Risk Assessment |
|---|---|
| Finds possible risks | Determines the level of risk |
| Identifies assets | Considers asset value |
| Identifies threats | Determines likelihood |
| Identifies vulnerabilities | Calculates/assigns risk |
| Answers **“What can go wrong?”** | Answers **“How serious is it?”** |

## Conclusion

**Risk Identification** finds the **assets, threats, and vulnerabilities**, while **Risk Assessment** determines the **likelihood and level of risk**. Together, they help an organization understand its security problems and decide which risks need priority protection.
