## Explain Minimum Viable Prototype and its Role in Product Development

### Introduction

A **Minimum Viable Prototype (MVP)** is the **simplest version of a proposed solution that contains enough features to test important assumptions and obtain meaningful user feedback**. It focuses only on the essential features required to evaluate the core idea. Design thinking unit 4 notes

### Characteristics of a Minimum Viable Prototype

A Minimum Viable Prototype:

1. **Contains only essential features** – Unnecessary features are avoided.
2. **Requires minimum resources** – Less time, money, and effort are required.
3. **Can be developed quickly** – The basic solution can be created in a short time.
4. **Tests critical assumptions** – It helps determine whether the main idea is valid.
5. **Allows early customer feedback** – Users can interact with the prototype and provide feedback.
6. **Supports rapid iteration** – The solution can be quickly modified based on the feedback. Design thinking unit 4 notes

---

## Role of Minimum Viable Prototype in Product Development

### 1. Reduces Development Cost
Only essential features are developed initially, so unnecessary development expenses can be avoided.

### 2. Reduces Risk
Important assumptions can be tested before investing heavily in the complete product.

### 3. Provides Early Market Feedback
Users can interact with the prototype and provide feedback before the final product is developed.

### 4. Identifies Unwanted Features
Feedback helps identify features that users do not need or value.

### 5. Supports Product-Market Validation
The prototype helps determine whether the proposed solution is suitable for the intended users and market. Design thinking unit 4 notes

### Example

Suppose a startup wants to develop an **online grocery application**.

Instead of developing the complete application initially, it can create a Minimum Viable Prototype containing only:

- Product catalogue
- Basic product selection
- Simple order placement
- Basic payment or order confirmation

The startup can then observe customer responses before investing in advanced features. Design thinking unit 4 notes

### Conclusion

A Minimum Viable Prototype helps teams **test important assumptions with minimum resources** before developing the complete product. It reduces cost and risk, provides early customer feedback, identifies unwanted features, and supports product-market validation. Therefore, it plays an important role in developing products efficiently and improving them through rapid feedback and iteration.
