## Explain the Define Phase in Design Thinking

### Definition

The **Define phase** is the **second stage of Design Thinking**. In this phase, designers analyze the information collected during the **Empathize phase** and clearly identify the actual problem that needs to be solved. The main outcome is a **clear and focused problem statement** that guides the Ideate phase. 

### Objectives of the Define Phase

1. **Analyze User Research** – Study and understand the information collected from users.
2. **Identify Root Causes** – Find the main reasons behind the user's problem.
3. **Organize User Insights** – Arrange research findings into meaningful patterns.
4. **Prioritize User Needs** – Identify the most important needs and problems.
5. **Develop a Problem Statement** – Clearly describe the problem from the user's perspective.
6. **Focus on the Correct Problem** – Ensure the team solves the actual user problem instead of an assumed problem. 

### Methods Used in the Define Phase

The main methods mentioned in the document are:

* **Storytelling** – Communicates user experiences through narratives and helps teams understand emotions and challenges.
* **Critical Items Diagram** – Organizes and prioritizes important insights from research.
* **Define Success** – Establishes measurable criteria for evaluating whether a solution meets its objectives. 

### Benefits of the Define Phase

* Provides a **clear direction** for the design process.
* Helps identify the **actual user problem**.
* Prevents the team from **solving the wrong problem**.
* Creates a focused problem statement for the **Ideate phase**. 

### Conclusion

The Define phase converts **user research and insights into a clear problem statement**. It ensures that the Design Thinking team has a proper understanding of the problem before moving to idea generation.
