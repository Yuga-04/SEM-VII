## Explain the Define Phase in Design Thinking

### Definition

The **Define phase** is the **second stage of Design Thinking**. During this phase, designers analyze the information and insights collected during the **Empathize phase** and clearly identify the actual problem that needs to be solved.

The main purpose of this phase is to convert user research into a **clear and focused problem statement**. This problem statement provides direction for the **Ideate phase** and ensures that the team focuses on solving the correct user problem.

### Objectives of the Define Phase

1. **Analyze User Research**
   
   Designers carefully study the information collected through interviews, observations, surveys, and other research methods to understand the user's problems and experiences.

2. **Identify Root Causes**
   
   The team looks beyond the visible problem and tries to identify the **main reasons or root causes** behind the user's difficulties.

3. **Organize User Insights**
   
   Research findings are organized into meaningful **patterns, themes, and categories** so that important insights can be identified easily.

4. **Prioritize User Needs**
   
   Designers identify and prioritize the **most important user needs, problems, and challenges** that require attention.

5. **Develop a Problem Statement**
   
   The findings are converted into a clear problem statement that describes the problem from the **user's perspective**.

6. **Focus on the Correct Problem**
   
   The Define phase ensures that the team solves the **actual problem faced by users** rather than making assumptions or solving the wrong problem.

### Methods Used in the Define Phase

The main methods mentioned in the document are:

1. **Storytelling**
   
   Storytelling communicates user experiences through **narratives**. It helps the team understand the user's emotions, challenges, motivations, and needs in an easy-to-understand way.

2. **Critical Items Diagram**
   
   A Critical Items Diagram is a visual tool used to **organize and prioritize important observations, issues, and insights** collected during user research. It helps the team identify the most significant problems that should be addressed first.

3. **Define Success**
   
   Define Success involves establishing **clear and measurable criteria** to determine whether the proposed solution effectively addresses the identified problem. It also helps the team maintain a common understanding of the desired outcomes.

### Activities in the Define Phase

The major activities performed during this phase include:

- **Analyzing research findings**
- **Organizing and grouping insights**
- **Identifying patterns and root causes**
- **Prioritizing important user needs**
- **Framing the problem clearly**
- **Developing a focused problem statement**
- **Defining success criteria**

### Benefits of the Define Phase

- Provides a **clear direction** for the design process.
- Helps identify the **actual user problem**.
- Prevents the team from **solving the wrong problem**.
- Organizes large amounts of research into useful insights.
- Helps prioritize the **most important user needs**.
- Creates a focused problem statement for the **Ideate phase**.
- Supports better **decision-making and innovation**.

### Conclusion

The **Define phase** converts user research and insights gathered during the Empathize phase into a **clear and focused problem statement**. By analyzing research, identifying root causes, organizing insights, and prioritizing user needs, the team can understand the correct problem. Thus, the Define phase provides a strong foundation for the **Ideate phase and the development of user-centered solutions**.
