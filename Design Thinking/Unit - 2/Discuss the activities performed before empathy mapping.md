## Discuss the Activities Performed Before Empathy Mapping

### Introduction

Before creating an **Empathy Map**, designers need to collect and organize reliable information about users. Proper preparation ensures that the empathy map represents the **actual experiences, feelings, motivations, behaviors, and expectations** of users rather than assumptions. The document identifies several activities that should be completed before empathy mapping.

### Activities Performed Before Empathy Mapping

1. **Define the Objective**
   
   The first step is to clearly identify the **purpose of the study** and the problem that needs to be explored. This provides a clear direction for the research.

2. **Identify Target Users**
   
   Designers must identify and select the appropriate **user group or customer segment** for the study. This ensures that the collected information is relevant to the target audience.

3. **Conduct User Research**
   
   Information about users should be collected using different research methods such as:
   - **Interviews**
   - **Surveys**
   - **Questionnaires**
   - **Observation**
   - **Focus groups**
   - **Contextual inquiry**

4. **Observe Users in Real Environments**
   
   Designers should observe users while they perform their **actual tasks in their natural environment**. This helps understand their natural behavior and experiences.

5. **Prepare Interview Questions**
   
   Designers should prepare **open-ended questions** that encourage users to explain their experiences, problems, needs, and expectations in detail.

6. **Collect Qualitative Data**
   
   Designers collect information about important user aspects such as:
   - Feelings
   - Motivations
   - Challenges
   - Behaviors
   - Expectations

7. **Record User Observations**
   
   The information collected during research should be properly documented using **notes, photographs with permission, audio recordings, or videos** for later analysis.

8. **Remove Assumptions**
   
   Designers should avoid making decisions based on their **personal opinions, assumptions, or previous beliefs**. The empathy map should be based on actual user information.

9. **Group Similar Findings**
   
   Similar research findings are organized into common **themes or patterns**. Techniques such as **affinity mapping or clustering** can be used for this purpose.

10. **Select Representative Users**
    
    Designers should select users who **accurately represent the target audience**. This helps ensure that the empathy map reflects the experiences of the intended users.

11. **Prepare Team Members**
    
    All team members should understand the **research objectives** and follow consistent research methods. This creates a common approach among the team.

12. **Gather Supporting Materials**
    
    Additional information such as **customer feedback, product reviews, support tickets, usage analytics, and previous research reports** should be collected when available.

### Importance of These Activities

These preparation activities help designers collect **accurate and meaningful user information** before creating the empathy map. They reduce assumptions, reveal user needs and behaviors, and help the team identify important patterns and insights.

### Conclusion

The activities performed before empathy mapping ensure that the empathy map is based on **reliable user research rather than assumptions**. By defining objectives, identifying users, conducting research, observing users, recording findings, grouping information, and preparing the team, designers can create an accurate empathy map that supports **user-centered Design Thinking**.
