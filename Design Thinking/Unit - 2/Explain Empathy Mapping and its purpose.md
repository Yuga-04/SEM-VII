## Explain Empathy Mapping and its Purpose

### Definition

**Empathy Mapping** is a visual tool used to organize and understand information collected from users. It helps designers understand what users **think, feel, say, and do**, providing a complete view of the user's experience. 

### Components of an Empathy Map

1. **Says**
   Represents what the user says during interviews, including their statements and quotes.
   *Example:* “This app is confusing.”

2. **Thinks**
   Represents what the user thinks but may not openly express, including their concerns, beliefs, and expectations.
   *Example:* “There must be an easier way.”

3. **Does**
   Represents the user's actual actions and behaviors.
   *Example:* Frequently checking an application or asking others for help.

4. **Feels**
   Represents the user's emotions and attitudes, such as **frustration, happiness, confusion, or excitement**. 

### Purpose of Empathy Mapping

1. **Understand Users Better** – Organizes user information into meaningful categories.
2. **Visualize User Experience** – Shows users' emotions, behaviors, and experiences clearly.
3. **Identify User Needs** – Helps identify functional, emotional, and social needs.
4. **Discover Pain Points** – Highlights frustrations, obstacles, and challenges faced by users.
5. **Support Human-Centered Design** – Ensures design decisions are based on actual user experiences.
6. **Improve Team Communication** – Creates a common understanding among team members.
7. **Guide Product Development** – Helps prioritize features according to user requirements.
8. **Generate Better Ideas** – User insights help designers develop creative solutions during the Ideation phase. 

### Conclusion

Empathy Mapping helps designers **understand users deeply and identify their needs, emotions, behaviors, and pain points**. Therefore, it supports better decision-making and the development of effective user-centered solutions.
