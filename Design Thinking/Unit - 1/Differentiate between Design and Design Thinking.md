# Differentiate Between Design and Design Thinking – 13 Marks

**Design** and **Design Thinking** are closely related, but they are not the same. Design mainly focuses on **creating a product, service or system**, whereas Design Thinking focuses on **understanding the problem and developing a human-centered solution**. 

| **Aspect**                | **Design**                                                   | **Design Thinking**                                                           |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Primary focus**      | Focuses on creating or shaping a product, service or system. | Focuses on understanding the problem and developing human-centered solutions. |
| **2. Starting point**     | May begin with specifications or requirements.               | Begins by exploring users, context and needs.                                 |
| **3. Problem definition** | The problem may already be given as part of the design task. | The team investigates and reframes the problem when necessary.                |
| **4. User involvement**   | User involvement depends on the design process.              | Understanding the user is central to the process.                             |
| **5. Idea generation**    | May focus on selected design alternatives.                   | Encourages exploration of many alternatives.                                  |
| **6. Prototyping**        | May be used during product development.                      | Used as an important learning and experimentation tool.                       |
| **7. Testing**            | Testing can occur at defined stages.                         | Feedback and testing are continuously integrated into the process.            |
| **8. Nature**             | Can be more **solution-focused**.                            | Is **human-centered, iterative and exploratory**.                             |
| **9. Approach**           | Mainly concerned with developing the required solution.      | First understands people and the actual problem before developing a solution. |
| **10. Outcome**           | Mainly results in a designed product, service or system.     | Aims to create an appropriate solution that satisfies user needs.             |

The document gives a simple way to remember the difference:

**Design:** *“What can we create to meet the requirement?”*

**Design Thinking:** *“What is the real human problem, and how can we create and validate an appropriate solution?”* 

### Similarities

* Both aim to create **effective solutions**.
* Both involve **creativity and innovation**. 

### Conclusion

In simple terms, **Design is mainly about creating a solution**, while **Design Thinking is about understanding the user and solving the right problem creatively**. Therefore, Design Thinking provides a broader, human-centered and iterative approach to problem-solving.
