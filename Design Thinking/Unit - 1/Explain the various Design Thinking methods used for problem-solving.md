# Various Design Thinking Methods Used for Problem-Solving – 13 Marks

Design Thinking uses different **practical methods and techniques** to understand users, identify problems, generate ideas, develop solutions and obtain feedback. The main methods given in the document are **Observation, Interviews, Surveys, Brainstorming, Mind Mapping, Prototyping and User Testing**. 

## 1. Observation

**Observation** means studying users and their behavior in real situations.

It helps designers:

* Understand how users actually behave.
* Identify difficulties and problems.
* Discover needs that users may not directly express.

**Example:** Observing students using a college library to identify difficulties in finding books or using resources. 

## 2. Interviews

**Interviews** involve directly interacting with users and asking questions about their experiences, needs and difficulties.

They help collect **detailed information** that may not be obtained through observation alone. 

## 3. Surveys

**Surveys** are used to collect feedback and information from a **large group of users**.

They help designers understand common opinions, preferences and problems among different users. 

## 4. Brainstorming

**Brainstorming** is a group creativity technique used to generate a **large number of ideas without criticism or judgment**.

It encourages team members to think freely and develop different possible solutions to a problem. 

## 5. Mind Mapping

**Mind Mapping** is a visual method for **organizing and connecting ideas**.

A central problem or topic is placed at the center, and related ideas are connected around it. This helps the team explore different aspects of a problem and generate new ideas. 

## 6. Prototyping

**Prototyping** means creating a sample or early model of a proposed solution.

Examples include:

* Paper sketches
* Wireframes
* Mock-ups
* Physical models
* Digital prototypes

It helps designers make ideas tangible, identify problems early and make improvements before final development. 

## 7. User Testing

**User Testing** involves allowing users to evaluate a proposed solution or prototype.

It helps designers:

* Collect user feedback.
* Identify usability problems.
* Understand strengths and weaknesses.
* Improve the solution.



## Benefits of Design Thinking Methods

These methods help in:

* Better understanding of users.
* Identifying real problems.
* Generating creative solutions.
* Improving innovation.
* Making better decisions.
* Developing user-friendly solutions. 

## Conclusion

Thus, Design Thinking methods provide practical ways to **understand users, explore ideas, create solutions and improve them through feedback**. Using methods such as **Observation, Interviews, Surveys, Brainstorming, Mind Mapping, Prototyping and User Testing** makes problem-solving more effective and user-centered.
