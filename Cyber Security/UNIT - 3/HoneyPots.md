# Honeypots – 13 Mark Answer

## 1. Definition

A **honeypot** is a network-attached system designed as a **trap for cyber attackers**. It appears to be a legitimate target but is actually monitored by security teams to detect and study unauthorized activities.

Honeypots help cybersecurity researchers understand the **techniques and types of attacks** used by hackers. 

---

## 2. Purpose of Honeypots

The main purposes of honeypots are:

* To **detect unauthorized access** and attacks.
* To study attackers' **methods and techniques**.
* To collect information about cyber attacks.
* To provide security researchers with knowledge about new threats.
* To act as an additional layer of security for an organization. 

---

## 3. Types of Honeypots

### A. Based on Deployment

#### 1. Research Honeypots

These are mainly used by **security researchers** to study hacker attacks and develop methods to prevent them. 

#### 2. Production Honeypots

These are deployed within **production networks along with real servers**. They act as a frontend trap to attract attackers while protecting the actual production systems. 

---

## 4. How a Honeypot Works

The basic working process is:

**Deployment → Attract Attacker → Detect Attack → Collect Information → Analyze → Improve Security**

1. A honeypot is deployed as an apparently useful system or resource.
2. An attacker interacts with the honeypot.
3. The security team detects the unauthorized activity.
4. Information about the attacker's actions is collected.
5. Researchers analyze the collected information.
6. The findings are used to develop better security measures.

---

## 5. Advantages of Honeypots

* Helps detect attacks that may bypass normal security controls.
* Provides information about **attacker behavior**.
* Helps researchers understand new attack techniques.
* Can identify unauthorized access attempts.
* Provides useful information for improving cybersecurity defenses.

---

## 6. Limitations of Honeypots

* **Cost:** Setting up a realistic honeypot can require specialized resources and skills.
* **Maintenance:** It needs proper monitoring and management.
* **Risk:** If incorrectly configured, attackers may use it as a starting point for further attacks.
* **Limited visibility:** A honeypot mainly provides information about attacks directed toward it.
* Attackers may sometimes identify that a system is a honeypot.

The document also notes that honeypots generally require **specialized skills and resources** and can be expensive to implement. 

---

## 7. Importance in Cybersecurity

Honeypots are particularly useful for organizations and cybersecurity researchers because they provide a controlled environment for observing attackers. The collected information helps organizations understand **how attacks occur and how future attacks can be prevented**.

## Conclusion

A **honeypot is a decoy system used to attract, detect and study cyber attackers**. Research and production honeypots serve different purposes, but both help organizations improve their security by providing valuable information about attacker behavior and attack techniques.
