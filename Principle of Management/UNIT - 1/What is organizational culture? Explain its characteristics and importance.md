# Organizational Culture – 13 Mark Answer

## 1. Meaning of Organizational Culture

**Organizational culture** refers to the **psychology, attitudes, experiences, beliefs and values** of an organization. It is the collection of **values and norms shared by people and groups** that guides how they interact with each other and with outside stakeholders. 

In simple words, **organizational culture means “the way things are done in an organization.”**

---

## 2. Characteristics of Organizational Culture

### 1. Shared Values and Beliefs

Culture consists of common **values, beliefs and attitudes** shared by organizational members. 

### 2. Guides Behaviour

It controls and guides how employees **behave and interact** with one another and outsiders.

### 3. Develops Through Practices

Daily **rituals and routines** indicate what behaviour is acceptable and what management values. 

### 4. Reflected Through Symbols

Company **logos, office arrangements and dress codes** can represent its culture. 

### 5. Influenced by Structure and Power

Organizational structure and the people who have real power influence the culture and decision-making process. 

### 6. Changes Over Time

Changing organizational culture requires changes in **policies, workplace ethics and management systems** and can affect the overall output. 

---

## 3. Importance of Organizational Culture

1. **Guides employee behaviour** – It helps employees understand acceptable behaviour.
2. **Creates common values** – Shared values bring employees together.
3. **Improves teamwork** – Common beliefs and practices encourage cooperation.
4. **Improves communication** – A common culture helps employees understand each other.
5. **Increases employee satisfaction** – A positive work environment can improve morale and satisfaction.
6. **Supports organizational goals** – Culture influences how employees work towards organizational objectives.
7. **Helps in managing change** – A suitable culture can help employees accept organizational changes.
8. **Provides consistency** – A strong culture creates consistent practices and behaviour. The notes particularly describe process culture as providing consistency through adherence to laws and procedures. 

---

## 4. Elements of Organizational Culture

According to **Johnson and Scholes' cultural web**, the six important elements are:

1. **Stories** – Stories about important past events and people.
2. **Rituals and Routines** – Regular activities showing acceptable behaviour.
3. **Symbols** – Logos, office design and dress codes.
4. **Organizational Structure** – Formal and informal power relationships.
5. **Control Systems** – Financial, quality and reward systems.
6. **Power Structures** – Individuals or groups having major influence over decisions. 

### Conclusion

Organizational culture is an important part of management because it shapes **employee behaviour, values, relationships and working methods**. A suitable culture creates a positive environment and helps the organization achieve its objectives effectively.
