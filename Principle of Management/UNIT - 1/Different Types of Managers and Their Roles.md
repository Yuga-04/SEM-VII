# Different Types of Managers and Their Roles – 13 Marks

## 1. Introduction

A **manager** is a person who gets work done through others to achieve organizational goals. Based on the level of authority, managers are classified into **Top, Middle and Lower-level managers**. 

### 2. Top-Level Managers

Top management includes **Board of Directors, CEO and Managing Director**. They mainly deal with planning and coordination. 

**Roles:**

* Set organizational objectives and broad policies.
* Prepare strategic plans.
* Give instructions for budgets, procedures and schedules.
* Appoint middle-level managers.
* Control and coordinate all departments.
* Maintain contact with the outside world.
* Provide guidance and direction.
* Take responsibility for overall organizational performance. 

### 3. Middle-Level Managers

Middle management includes **departmental and branch managers**. They are responsible for implementing the plans of top management. 

**Roles:**

* Implement plans according to top management policies.
* Prepare plans for their departments.
* Participate in recruitment and training.
* Explain top-level policies to lower managers.
* Coordinate departmental activities.
* Send reports to top management.
* Evaluate and motivate junior managers. 

### 4. Lower-Level Managers

Lower management is also called **supervisory or operative management**. It includes **supervisors, foremen and section officers**. 

**Roles:**

* Assign jobs to workers.
* Guide and supervise workers.
* Maintain production quality and quantity.
* Communicate workers' problems and suggestions.
* Solve worker grievances.
* Arrange materials, machines and tools.
* Provide training and maintain discipline.
* Prepare performance reports.
* Motivate workers. 

### 5. Managerial Roles – Henry Mintzberg

Mintzberg classified managerial roles into **three categories**: 

**A. Interpersonal Roles**

* **Figurehead** – Symbolic head of the organization.
* **Leader** – Handles staffing and training.
* **Liaison** – Maintains communication with contacts. 

**B. Informational Roles**

* **Monitor** – Collects important information.
* **Disseminator** – Shares information with employees.
* **Spokesperson** – Communicates organizational information to outsiders. 

**C. Decisional Roles**

* **Entrepreneur** – Searches for opportunities and change.
* **Disturbance Handler** – Takes corrective action during problems.
* **Resource Allocator** – Allocates organizational resources.
* **Negotiator** – Represents the organization in negotiations. 

### Conclusion

Thus, **top managers plan, middle managers implement and coordinate, and lower managers supervise and control**. Together, they ensure the achievement of organizational objectives.
