# Centralization and Decentralization – 13 Mark Answer

## 1. Introduction

**Centralization and decentralization** are two opposite approaches to distributing **decision-making authority** in an organization. Centralization concentrates authority at higher levels, whereas decentralization transfers authority to lower levels. According to **Henry Fayol**, there should be a proper balance between the two and extreme centralization or decentralization should be avoided. 

---

## 2. Centralization

### Definition

**Centralization** is the process of transferring and assigning **decision-making authority to higher levels** of the organizational hierarchy. In this system, top managers have greater control over important decisions. 

### Characteristics of Centralization

* Emphasis is on **top-down control, leadership, vision and strategy**.
* Decision-making is generally **strong and authoritative**.
* Organizational changes are mainly directed by top management.
* Execution is **fast, coordinated and decisive**.
* There is greater **uniformity** in decisions and policies. 

### Advantages of Centralization

1. **Power and prestige to managers** – Higher-level managers have greater authority.
2. **Uniformity** – Policies, practices and decisions remain consistent throughout the organization.
3. **Less control procedures** – Extensive controlling procedures are minimized.
4. **Avoids duplication** – Duplication of functions can be reduced. 

### Disadvantages of Centralization

1. **Neglect of middle-level functions** – Middle-level managers may have limited authority.
2. **Lower motivation** – Employees and lower-level managers may feel less motivated because they have little participation in decision-making. 

**Example:** In a small company, major decisions such as purchasing, finance and recruitment may be made only by the owner or top manager.

---

## 3. Decentralization

### Definition

**Decentralization** is the process of transferring and assigning **decision-making authority to lower levels** of the organizational hierarchy. Lower-level managers have greater autonomy in making decisions. 

### Characteristics of Decentralization

* Emphasis is on **bottom-up participation**.
* Decision-making is more **democratic and participative**.
* Changes can emerge from interactions within the organization.
* Execution is **flexible** and can respond to minor issues quickly.
* It promotes **participation and accountability**. 

### Forms of Decentralization

1. **De-concentration:** Decision-making authority is redistributed to lower or regional levels of the same organization.
2. **Delegation:** Decision-making responsibility is transferred to semi-autonomous units that remain accountable to the central organization.
3. **Devolution:** Authority is completely transferred to autonomous organizational units. 

### Advantages of Decentralization

1. **Improves morale** and interpersonal relationships.
2. **Reduces the burden** of daily administration on top management.
3. Brings **decision-making closer to action**.
4. Helps in **developing second-line managers**.
5. Promotes **employee enthusiasm and coordination**.
6. Facilitates quick action by **lower-level managers**. 

### Disadvantages of Decentralization

1. **Reduced status of top management** may be felt by senior administrators.
2. Qualified personnel may not always be utilized to their maximum potential.
3. **Increased cost** because more managers and staff may be required.
4. May result in **overlapping and duplication of efforts**. 

**Example:** In a large company with different regional branches, branch managers may be given authority to make local sales, staffing and operational decisions.

---

## 4. Difference between Centralization and Decentralization

| Centralization                                    | Decentralization                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Authority is concentrated at higher levels.       | Authority is distributed to lower levels.         |
| Decision-making is mainly top-down.               | Decision-making is more participative.            |
| Lower-level managers have less autonomy.          | Lower-level managers have greater autonomy.       |
| Provides greater uniformity.                      | Provides greater flexibility.                     |
| Suitable when strong central control is required. | Suitable when quick local decisions are required. |

---

## 5. Conclusion

Centralization provides **uniformity, control and coordinated decision-making**, while decentralization provides **participation, flexibility and faster decisions at lower levels**. Therefore, an organization should maintain a **proper balance between centralization and decentralization** according to its size, nature and requirements. Fayol specifically emphasized that extreme forms of either should be avoided.
