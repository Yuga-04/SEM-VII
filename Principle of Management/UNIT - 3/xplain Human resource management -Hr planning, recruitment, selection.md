# Human Resource Management – HR Planning, Recruitment and Selection

### 13 Mark Answer

## 1. Introduction

**Human Resource Management (HRM)** is concerned with managing the people required for achieving organizational objectives. In the given notes, this function is mainly covered under **staffing**, which involves filling organizational positions with competent and qualified persons. The staffing process includes **manpower planning, recruitment, selection and training**. 

---

## 2. Human Resource Planning (HR Planning)

### Definition

**Human Resource Planning**, also called **Manpower Planning**, means putting the **right number and right kind of people at the right place and at the right time** to achieve organizational goals. Its main purpose is to analyze the human resources available and determine the personnel required for different positions. 

### Main Activities of HR Planning

* Analyse the existing human resources.
* Determine the number of employees required.
* Identify the type and qualifications of employees needed.
* Match employees with suitable positions.
* Plan manpower requirements for different levels, from workers to executives.

**Example:** If a company plans to open a new branch, HR planning determines how many managers, technicians and other employees are required.

---

## 3. Recruitment

### Definition

**Recruitment** is the process of **finding and attracting suitable candidates** who are capable of filling job vacancies. Job description and job specification help identify the nature of the job and qualifications required. 

### Recruitment Process

**1. Identification of Vacancy**
The HR department receives a recruitment request containing the post to be filled, number of persons required, duties and qualifications. 

**2. Preparation of Job Description and Job Specification**

* **Job Description:** Describes the duties, responsibilities and nature of the job.
* **Job Specification:** Describes the education, experience, skills, knowledge and abilities required. 

**3. Selection of Sources**

Recruitment sources are of two types:

* **Internal sources:** Transfers and promotions within the organization.
* **External sources:** Outsourcing agencies and other sources outside the organization. 

**4. Advertising the Vacancy**
Vacancies may be advertised through **newspapers, television, radio, internet, direct mail**, etc. 

**5. Managing the Response**
Applications are received and resumes are screened according to the job requirements. Suitable candidates are called for the next stage. 

---

## 4. Selection

### Definition

**Selection** is the process of choosing the most suitable candidate from the applicants. Selecting the right candidate is important because organizational success largely depends on its employees and proper selection helps reduce employee turnover. 

### Selection Process

**1. Initial Screening**
Applications are examined and unqualified candidates are eliminated. 

**2. Preliminary Interview**
Candidates are checked for minimum eligibility, skills, academic background, competencies and interests. 

**3. Application Form**
Eligible candidates fill in a formal application containing personal and professional details such as qualifications and experience. 

**4. Personal Interview**
The employer obtains additional information and evaluates the candidate's communication ability and suitability for the job. 

**5. Reference Check**
References are contacted to obtain information about the candidate's behaviour, skills and conduct. 

**6. Background Verification**
The information provided by the candidate is verified. 

**7. Final Interview and Tests**
The candidate is evaluated through final interviews and suitable tests to determine capabilities, behaviour and special qualities. 

**8. Physical Examination**
A physical examination may be conducted to check the candidate's health. 

**9. Job Offer**
Candidates who successfully complete the selection stages are given the job offer. 

### Selection Flow

**Vacancy → HR Planning → Recruitment → Screening → Interview → Verification/Tests → Final Selection → Job Offer**

---

## 5. Conclusion

Human Resource Management ensures that an organization has **competent and qualified employees**. HR planning identifies the manpower required, recruitment attracts suitable candidates, and selection chooses the best candidate for the job. Thus, these activities help the organization place the **right person in the right job** and achieve its objectives effectively.
