# Training and Development, Performance Management and Career Planning

### 13 Mark Answer

## 1. Introduction

Human Resource Management does not end with recruitment and selection. Employees must be **trained, developed, evaluated and guided in their careers**. These activities improve employee performance and help the organization meet its future needs. The given PDF discusses these areas mainly through **training and development, performance appraisal and career development**. 

---

## 2. Training

### Definition

**Training** is the process of learning a sequence of programmed behaviour. It improves an employee's performance in the **current job** and prepares the employee for an intended job. 

### Objectives of Training

1. **Improve productivity** – Better skills increase operational productivity.
2. **Improve quality** – Trained employees make fewer operational mistakes.
3. **Improve organizational climate** – Better performance improves employee morale.
4. **Improve health and safety** – Proper training helps prevent industrial accidents.
5. **Personal growth** – Training increases employees' skills and awareness. 

### Training Process

1. **Identify training needs** – Determine organizational, operational and employee skill requirements.
2. **Getting ready for the job** – Prepare the trainer and learner and familiarize the learner with equipment and work.
3. **Presentation of operation and knowledge** – Explain and demonstrate the required knowledge and operations.
4. **Performance try-out** – The trainee performs the job repeatedly to develop skill and confidence.
5. **Follow-up** – Evaluate the effectiveness of the training programme. 

### Training Methods

* **On-the-job training** – Learning while working with experienced employees.
* **Off-the-job training** – Learning away from the actual workplace through lectures, seminars, workshops, case studies, role play, etc. 

---

## 3. Development

### Definition

**Development** is concerned with improving employees for **future jobs and responsibilities**. It focuses on imparting knowledge, changing attitudes and increasing skills. Its major objective is managerial effectiveness through planned learning. 

### Development Process

1. **Setting development objectives**
2. **Ascertaining development needs**
3. **Determining development needs** through appraisal of present management talent and manpower inventory.
4. **Conducting development programmes** according to individual and organizational needs.
5. **Programme evaluation** to assess the value of training and development. 

**Difference:** Training mainly improves performance in the **present job**, whereas development prepares employees for **future responsibilities**. 

---

## 4. Performance Management / Performance Appraisal

The PDF explains performance management mainly through **Performance Appraisal**.

### Definition

**Performance appraisal** is the process of obtaining, analysing and recording information about the relative worth of an employee. It measures and improves the employee's **actual performance and future potential**. 

### Objectives

* Review employee performance over a given period.
* Identify the gap between **actual and desired performance**.
* Help management exercise organizational control.
* Improve communication between managers and employees.
* Identify training and development needs.
* Provide feedback to employees.
* Assist in personnel decisions.
* Clarify employee expectations and responsibilities.
* Evaluate the effectiveness of recruitment, selection, training and development.
* Reduce employee grievances. 

### Performance Appraisal Process

1. **Establish performance standards** – Set clear and measurable standards.
2. **Communicate the standards** – Explain the standards and expectations to employees.
3. **Measure actual performance** – Compare employee performance with the established standards.
4. **Compare actual performance with standards** – Identify performance gaps.
5. **Take corrective action and provide feedback** – Improve performance wherever required.

The first two stages are specifically described in the PDF as setting and communicating performance standards.

## 5. Career Planning

**Career planning** involves guiding employees towards suitable career growth and future responsibilities. The PDF explains career development as a process that improves job performance and contributes to the growth of an individual's potential and personality. 

### Career Development Process

1. **Set development objectives** – Identify future career and organizational requirements.
2. **Ascertain development needs** – Forecast present and future growth requirements.
3. **Determine development needs** – Identify skill deficiencies through appraisal and manpower inventory.
4. **Conduct development programmes** – Provide suitable learning and development opportunities.
5. **Evaluate the programme** – Assess whether development activities meet organizational objectives. 

### Career Stages

The PDF identifies different career stages:

* **Trial Stage** – Employee explores career options and different jobs.
* **Establishment/Advancement Stage** – Focus is on achievement, performance and career growth.
* **Mid-Career Crisis** – Employee reassesses progress against career ambitions.
* **Maintenance Stage** – Employee maintains the established position and may need technical updating.
* **Late-Career Stage** – Employee prepares for retirement and develops interests outside work. 

---

## 6. Conclusion

**Training and development** improve employee knowledge and skills, **performance management** measures and improves employee performance, and **career planning** supports future growth. Together, these HR activities help employees perform better and enable the organization to develop capable personnel for its present and future needs.
