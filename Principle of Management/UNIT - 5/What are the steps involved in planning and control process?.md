# Steps Involved in Planning and Control Process – 13 Marks

Planning and controlling are closely related functions of management. **Planning decides what is to be done, while controlling checks whether the planned activities are being achieved.**

## A. Planning Process

The PDF gives the following steps in the planning process: POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 1. Perception of Opportunities
The first step is to identify and understand possible future opportunities. The organization should assess its **strengths, weaknesses, uncertainties and expected benefits**.

### 2. Establishing Objectives
Objectives are established for the **entire enterprise and each subordinate unit**. They specify the results to be achieved and provide direction for further planning.

### 3. Considering the Planning Premises
Planning premises are the **assumptions about the future environment** in which plans will operate. They include forecasts relating to prices, costs, production, markets and other relevant factors.

### 4. Identification of Alternatives
Managers should identify as many possible **alternative courses of action** as possible for achieving the objectives. The alternatives are then examined to find the most promising ones. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 5. Evaluation of Alternatives
Each alternative is evaluated by considering its **advantages, disadvantages, risks, profitability, cost and suitability** in relation to organizational objectives.

### 6. Choice of Alternative Plans
After evaluating the alternatives, management selects the plan that is most suitable for achieving the organizational objectives.

### 7. Formulation of Supporting Plans
The selected plan is supported by **departmental plans and budgets**. Budgets help convert plans into measurable financial terms and also provide standards for measuring progress. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 8. Establishing Sequence of Activities
The activities required to implement the plan are arranged in the proper **sequence and order**. This ensures that the plans can be effectively put into operation. POM FULL NOTES(5)

# B. Control Process

The PDF explains that controlling involves measuring accomplishment against standards and correcting deviations. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 1. Establishment of Standards
Standards are established as **yardsticks** against which actual performance can be measured.

### 2. Measurement of Actual Performance
The actual performance of the organization or employees is measured.

### 3. Comparison with Standards
Actual performance is compared with the established standards to determine whether there is any **deviation**.

### 4. Corrective Action
If deviations are found, suitable **corrective action** is taken to bring performance back to the required standard. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### Conclusion

Thus, **planning provides the objectives and course of action**, while **control measures actual performance, identifies deviations and takes corrective action**. Together, planning and control help the organization achieve its objectives effectively and efficiently.
