# Productivity and Methods of Improving Productivity in IT Industry – 13 Marks

## 1. Meaning of Productivity

**Productivity refers to the ratio between the output from production processes and its input.** It is a measure of the technical or engineering efficiency of production. POM FULL NOTES(5)

\[
\boxed{\text{Productivity}=\frac{\text{Output}}{\text{Input}}}
\]

In the IT industry, productivity can be related to the amount of software or functionality delivered compared with the resources such as **time, effort and cost** used to produce it.

---

## 2. Types of Productivity in IT

### a) Physical Productivity
It is the ratio of the **amount of product to the resources consumed**, usually effort.

In software development, output can be measured in:
- Lines of code
- Classes
- Screens

Effort can be measured in:
- Staff hours
- Staff days
- Staff months POM FULL NOTES(5)

### b) Functional Productivity
It is the ratio of the **functionality delivered to the resources consumed**.

Functionality may be measured using:
- Use cases
- Requirements
- Features
- Function points POM FULL NOTES(5)

### c) Economic Productivity
It is the ratio of the **value of the product produced to the cost of resources used**.

\[
\boxed{\text{Economic Productivity}=\frac{\text{Value}}{\text{Cost}}}
\]

It helps evaluate the economic efficiency of an organization and is useful in decisions regarding outsourcing and subcontracting. POM FULL NOTES(5)

# 3. Methods of Improving Productivity in IT Industry

### 1. Proper Planning of Work
Work should be thoroughly planned so that activities are performed systematically and bottlenecks are avoided. The notes emphasize thorough planning as a means of achieving higher productivity. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 2. Selection of Skilled Employees
Employees should be selected according to the requirements of the job. Proper selection ensures that people with suitable knowledge and skills are assigned to appropriate tasks.

### 3. Training and Development
Training improves employee performance and productivity. Properly trained employees make fewer mistakes and develop better skills, speed and confidence. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 4. Specialization
Work should be divided among employees according to their specialization. Division of work and specialization can lead to maximum productivity and efficiency. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 5. Standardization
Standard methods, tools, equipment and working procedures should be used. Standardization creates uniformity and improves efficiency. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 6. Use of Modern Technology
Computers and modern IT tools can make management-science techniques more practical. Technology can support planning, scheduling, quality control and computer-integrated operations. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 7. Work Measurement
Productivity should be measured regularly. In IT projects, measures such as staff hours, staff days, lines of code, features and function points can be used to evaluate productivity. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 8. Elimination of Unnecessary Work
Methods and motion studies can be used to examine how work is performed and eliminate unnecessary activities or movements. This improves operational efficiency. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 9. Proper Scheduling
Effective scheduling helps complete activities in the required time. **PERT and Gantt charts** can be used to analyze tasks, project duration and dependencies. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 10. Improve Quality
Productivity should be improved without reducing quality. The notes identify **productivity and quality** as important areas of operations management. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 11. Employee Motivation
Employees should be encouraged to achieve higher performance. Taylor's approach emphasizes cooperation between management and workers and sharing the benefits of higher productivity. POM FULL NOTES(5)

### 12. Continuous Improvement
Organizations should continuously search for better methods of working, reduce waste and improve efficiency. Cost-control practices in the notes specifically mention continuous search for improvement as creating a climate for increased efficiency. POM FULL NOTES(5)

## Conclusion

Productivity is the **ratio of output to input** and is an important measure of efficiency. In the IT industry, productivity can be improved through **proper planning, skilled manpower, training, specialization, standardization, modern technology, work measurement, effective scheduling, quality improvement and continuous improvement**.
