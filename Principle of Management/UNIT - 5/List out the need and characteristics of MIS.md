### Meaning of MIS

**Management Information System (MIS)** is a system that collects, processes, stores and provides information to managers for **planning, decision-making, coordination and control** of organizational activities.

---

## A. Need / Importance of MIS

1. **Helps in decision-making**  
   MIS provides relevant and timely information to managers for making effective decisions.

2. **Supports planning**  
   It provides information about past and present performance, helping managers prepare future plans.

3. **Improves coordination**  
   MIS facilitates the flow of information between different departments and management levels.

4. **Effective control**  
   Managers can compare actual performance with planned standards and take corrective action.

5. **Improves efficiency**  
   Proper information helps reduce duplication, delays and wastage of organizational resources.

6. **Provides timely information**  
   MIS delivers the required information to managers at the right time.

7. **Helps identify problems**  
   Managers can detect deviations, problems and unfavorable trends at an early stage.

8. **Supports organizational growth**  
   Accurate information helps management understand business conditions and take suitable actions for growth.

---

## B. Characteristics of MIS

1. **Management-oriented**  
   MIS is designed according to the information needs of managers.

2. **Management-directed**  
   The management determines what information is required and how the system should operate.

3. **Integrated system**  
   MIS integrates information from different functional areas such as finance, marketing, production and human resources.

4. **Common database**  
   It uses a common and organized database to provide consistent information.

5. **Provides relevant information**  
   Information supplied by MIS should be relevant to the manager's requirements.

6. **Timely information**  
   Information must be available when it is required for decision-making.

7. **Accurate information**  
   MIS should provide reliable and accurate information.

8. **Useful for planning and control**  
   It supports managerial planning, performance monitoring and corrective actions.

9. **Computer-based system**  
   Modern MIS generally uses computers, databases and information technologies for processing information.

10. **Flexible system**  
    MIS should be capable of adapting to changing organizational requirements.

### Conclusion

MIS is an important management tool that converts organizational data into **useful information**. It helps managers in **planning, decision-making, coordination and control**, thereby improving organizational efficiency and effectiveness.
